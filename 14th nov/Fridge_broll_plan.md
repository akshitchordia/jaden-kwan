# Fridge reel: B-roll plan v1 (14 Nov 2026)

Base: `Fridge_zoom_v1_motionblur.mp4` (1387 frames, 57.85 s). Times are from the aligned transcript.

## Rules for this reel
- Look: "dorm room, 2 a.m.": the open fridge is the only light (cold blue-white) against warm dark. Red REC dot is the one accent colour. Halftone on every B-roll and graphic, never on the talking head. Serif type only.
- No B-roll on top of B-roll (the archival plays clean). Don't fill everything: the face carries the key lines.
- Wang only as paper cutouts from real photos. No generated photoreal Wang.
- Real screenshots for the Product Hunt page and his July post. I animate them; I don't recreate them.
- The fridge bookends the video: the hook fridge comes back at the very end ("full circle").
