@alexandr_wang (Alexandr Wang), 15 Jul 2026, 05:48 UTC (11:18 AM IST). https://x.com/alexandr_wang/status/2077269031078519110
> muse spark can refill your fridge for you!
>
> full circle, this problem was actual the inspiration for scale ai

("actual" is his typo.) He's quoting @tjshibata (Tyler), 14 Jul 2026:
> Muse Spark 1.1 can use browsers and it refilled our office fridge
> - Took 2 pics of our fridge stocked / not stocked
> - Told Muse to analyze the pics and restock what we were low on
> - It analyzed each shelf and which brand / items that were missing
> - Used the @juliusai built in browser to go to instacart, and add the items from target
> - Checked out and even left a tip for the driver
>
> One prompt + two pics to keep our team happy

## Gaps and flags
- Item 1: I couldn't find a 2015–2017 head-and-shoulders photo of Wang at 1000 px or more that's free and has no watermark. Getty and Bloomberg have editorial shots from 2019 onward, but those previews are watermarked and rights-managed.
- Item 7: there's no young Wang photo with a laptop, and none of the young full-body photos reach 1500 px.
- Item 14: no real MIT dorm photo with a mini fridge turned up in Commons or in Flickr's CC search. The best real MIT dorm set is NC-licensed.
- Item 6: the CVPR clips come from personal YouTube vlogs (©), shot vertically. I found no free or CC CVPR expo footage.
- Items 11 and 13: copyrighted third-party footage (BT21 Tesla Life; Cleo Abram). YouTube started returning bot-check/429 errors partway through, so the interview stills came from the official 4K clips Cleo Abram posted on X.
- Item 16 (FRIDGE_plate_ai) was not generated, as instructed.
