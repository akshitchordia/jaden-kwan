Real pixels, nothing else

Every frame here is built from the acquired assets and your own footage, in the reference's language: type behind people, cutouts, real pages as physical planes, light doing the highlighting. Every tag, label, placeholder and drawn stand-in from v2 is gone. The cut and zoom stay as locked. The hook is parked.

Taken out (the slop)

Little tags and labels: mono captions, confidence scores, "REC", file labels, corner text.
Drawn stand-ins: the code fridge, road, booths, avatar icons, silhouettes and cubes.
Fake UI: the "website inquiry" card, the dossier cards, the CCTV chrome.
Highlighter swipes and boxes on screenshots.
Captions that repeat the big type on the same frame.

The rules now

Real pixels only. If a shot needs a thing, it's a photo or footage of that thing.
Type is the only graphic. One bold grotesk, one serif italic, nothing else on screen.
Light does the pointing. A page brightens where the line is; nothing gets circled.
One idea per shot, 0.4–0.8 s quick cuts between real clips, a graphic hero frame per beat.
Every transition is motivated: flash on a number, glitch only on failure, whip on a change of direction.
One finish: heavy vignette, highlight bloom, fine grain, and every shot moves.
