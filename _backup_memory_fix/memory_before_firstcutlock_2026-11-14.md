Locked styles### 4a. TBH / Mochary caption lock: "flash-bloom" (17 Oct 2026)**Look*** Newsreader Medium 500 (opsz 16), about 82–100 px.* Tracking −0.028 em. White, upright.* No stroke, box or shadow.* Lowercase except names. No punctuation.* 1–4 words per line. Baseline about y 1415–1430.**Animation*** Each word blooms in from a 6 px blur with an 8× glow flash, 0.05 s before it's spoken.* A steady 4-layer glow (σ 3/10/24/72).* Lines cut with a 0.07 s fade.* No slide, no italic, never repeat words.**Key-word highlights:** e.g. advice, action, marketing, buyers.**Counters:** TBH "5,000,000" and Mochary "$10,000 / month" use rolling digit reels with motion blur and a lock bloom.**No captions where a graphic already says the words.**The full spec is in `Mochary\\\\\\\_project\\\\\\\_archive\\\\\\\\04\\\\\\\_Plans\\\\\\\_and\\\\\\\_notes\\\\\\\\Caption\\\\\\\_style\\\\\\\_lock\\\\\\\_flash\\\\\\\_bloom\\\\\\\_TBH\\\\\\\_and\\\\\\\_Mochary.md`.### 4b.
