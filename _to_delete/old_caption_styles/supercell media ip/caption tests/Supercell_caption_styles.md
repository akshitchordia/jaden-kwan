# Supercell: three caption-style tests (first 10 s)
None of these use the locked TBH/Mochary flash-bloom glow. Pick one, or mix parts, and I'll lock it as the new spec.

## A · Editorial serif
Reads like a magazine headline over the shot.

**Look**
- Font: Instrument Serif Regular, 122 px, sentence case, warm white (#F6F1E9).
- Story words switch to *Instrument Serif Italic*: killed, 30, five hits, Clash of Clans, Clash Royale, Ilkka Paananen, wild.

**Placement**
- Top of frame, in the dark shelf area above your head (baseline y ≈ 340).
- It never touches the face or the mic.

**Animation**
- Each word rises about 34 px out of an invisible mask line on its spoken onset, over 0.42 s with a quint ease-out.
- When the phrase ends, the line drifts up 10 px and fades out in 0.14 s.

**Shadow:** soft, offset 5 px, blur 12, about 50%. No glow.

**Chunking:** 2–4 words.

## B · Viral sans
The simple, high-retention "talking head" look.

**Look**
- Font: Inter ExtraBold, 92 px, sentence case, white.
- Numbers and the payoff get bigger: "30" at 1.45×, "five hits" at 1.3×.

**Placement:** centred low, at y ≈ 1600, in the lower third over the hand.

**Animation**
- Each bite pops from 0.80 to 1.00 scale with a slight overshoot in 0.20 s.
- Karaoke fill: words he hasn't said yet sit at 45% white, and each one fills to full white as he says it.

**Readability:** a thin dark keyline plus a soft drop shadow. No glow.

**Chunking:** 1–3 words. Very fast, one idea per beat.

## C · Field-notes mono
Feels like a documentary or an investigator's notes. It suits a "how a company really works" story.

**Look**
- Font: IBM Plex Mono Medium, 56 px, sentence case.
- Left-aligned in a narrow column, 14 characters wide, at x = 66.

**Placement:** bottom-left, beside the mic. The block grows upward from y ≈ 1560.

**Animation**
- Typed character by character in sync with the voice, with a blinking block cursor.
- Key facts (30, five hits, Clash of Clans, Clash Royale, Ilkka Paananen) get a paper-white highlight bar that wipes in behind them, and the text inverts to black.

**Readability:** a very soft dark plate behind the block. No glow.

**Chunking:** whole sentences. The block clears with a quick fade at each new sentence.
