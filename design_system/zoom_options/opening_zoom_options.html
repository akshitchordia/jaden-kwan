Opening zoom-out, six curves

Every reel starts with a curved zoom-out into the opening framing. Same frame, anchored on your face; only the curve changes. The graph under each one is scale over time, so you can see the shape of the move.

Previews step at 24 fps like the reel. Slow motion plays 4 times slower.

My pick: A, expo glide. It starts fast, so the first frame already has energy, then spends most of its time gliding in. It reads as smooth and premium without calling attention to itself.

In the real render each move gets the first-cut lock's motion blur (more blur while it's fast, none once it settles) and is cut from the full-res master, so even the 1.8x start stays sharp.
