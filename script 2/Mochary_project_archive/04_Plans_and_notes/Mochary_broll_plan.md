# Mochary: B-roll plan

The rules are the same as TBH:
- Cuts are fast (about 1 s) and abstract or cinematic, never literal stock. Halftone texture goes on later.
- Graphic overlays only where they add something.
- Punch lines stay on your face.
- **[GFX]** means I build it as a custom animation. **[FOOTAGE]** means you source a clip, using the Pinterest phrases as the look reference.
