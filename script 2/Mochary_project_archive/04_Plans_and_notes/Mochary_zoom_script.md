# Mochary: zoom script (talking head)

Scale is relative to the full frame (1.00 = wide). Every zoom is anchored on the face, so the face never moves on screen.

Moves:
- **CUT**: a hard jump cut to a new framing, placed in the pause before the phrase.
- **PUSH**: a slow push across the whole line, eased in and out.
- **EASE IN / EASE OUT**: a smooth eased zoom.
- **SNAP**: a fast eased zoom.

Fast moves have motion blur. The cuts at 0:29.9 and 0:52.3 sit exactly on the jump cuts already in your master.

The pattern (same as TBH):
- New sentences cut between wide and tight framings, so it reads like a two-camera shoot.
- Every line gets a slow push, so the frame is never dead.
- Numbers and punch phrases get a snap or an eased push-in: a billion dollars, 10 grand, the marketing, seven percent.
- Reveals and "zoom out to the big picture" lines get an eased zoom-out: the playbook is online, if your product just does the job.
- The last line builds and pushes in hardest on the payoff.
