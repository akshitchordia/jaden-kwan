# Surge (script 4): B-roll plan and references

Built from the same B-roll vocabulary as Mochary and Supercell (what has already gone live), applied to the Surge story.

**[GFX]** = I build it. **[FOOTAGE]** = real clips, film stills or a generated clip you source. The Pinterest searches are short on purpose: they find the look, not the exact shot.

---

## The art style we're carrying over

| Element | Where it worked before | Rule for Surge |
|---|---|---|
| Navy-black serif graphics | "The *Playbook*" list, "20 games killed" counter | Deep navy-black base, warm-white Instrument Serif, one italic word per title |
| Big rolling numbers | $1,000,000,000 (Mochary), 30+ (Supercell), YC "00 Demo Day" | Every number in the script gets one: $1.2B, 100, 12, 10, 1,000,000, $30B |
| Real footage of the real people | Altman/Huffman, Armstrong, Mochary interviews, Ilkka clips | Edwin Chen interview clips with a glowing name tag |
| 80s film-still office B-roll | The man on the phone through the blinds, the couch room | Sales floors, boardrooms, hallways; moody, grainy, never stock-bright |
| Sticker cutouts on simple diagrams | Ilkka on the "Cell" org chart, the reward cutouts | Edwin and the anonymous "researchers" as white-border stickers |
| Brand-colour full-bleed card | YC orange "Demo Day" counter | One card per company beat if needed, with a serif number |
| Constellation / dot map | The "like a marketplace" network, the night globe | The 12 buyers who all know each other |
| Halftone on every B-roll and graphic | Everything | Same. Never on your face |
| One accent colour | Amber for "alive", red for "killed" | **Electric blue** if the hook goes Breaking Bad (the blue product = Surge's data); otherwise amber. Red stays reserved for crossing things out |

**Timing rule:** every approved zoom snap (never, zero, Edwin, 100, no, sales, fight, distribution, $30) plays **on your face**. B-roll sits between snaps, enters off a word, and hands back to camera just before each snap.

**Story callbacks built into the plan:**
- The **empty boardroom** on "zero investors" comes back **full** on "investors want in".
- The **12-star constellation** of buyers who know each other comes back as a **million scattered dots** that never connect, on "distribution".
