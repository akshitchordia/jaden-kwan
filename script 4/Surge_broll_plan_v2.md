## The art direction: "Monochrome machine room"
What the reference does, and what we keep:
- **Black and white only.** Deep blacks, paper whites, high contrast. Colour appears at most once (the reference's cyan Earth), as an event.
- **One accent: thin red HUD lines.** Hairline tracking boxes and leader lines that lock onto things, like a machine reading the frame.
- **Dot-matrix and dither.** Shapes built from dots (the "singularity" ring), dithered photos, pixel-sort dissolves as transitions.
- **Inverted / negative photography.** Archival or classical images flipped to negative (the Michelangelo hands, the eye macro, the rocket).
- **Floating UI panels in 3D space** over a faint dark grid with dotted guide lines; the camera drifts through them.
- **Tiny lowercase mono labels** ("singularity", "one", "place") sitting next to things, never big.
- **Type:** our Instrument Serif stays for big words and numbers (the series identity); small labels in a mono (IBM Plex Mono).
- **Motion:** slow drifts and pushes, things light up / dissolve in; radial light streak for one big moment.

What you already have fits this: the dark techie montage (4.2–6.7), the paper node diagram (12.8), the depth-map walking people (14.2–15.5), and the B&W office archive (19–20.5). **One fix:** the Epic aerial (38–39.6) is in colour; I'll grade it to B&W and add a red HUD box and a mono label so it belongs.
