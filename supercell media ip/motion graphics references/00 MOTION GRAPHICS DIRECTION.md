# Supercell reel: motion graphics direction

**Folders:**
- Each numbered folder is one motion-graphics idea. Inside are a `00_board.jpg` (the whole idea at a glance), the individual reference images (01–08), and `sources.txt` (Pinterest links).
- `11 YouTube shot references` has thumbnails plus `links.md`: what to watch, how to build it, and which footage to screen-record.

---

## The overall direction: "Control room at night"
The story is a company quietly watching numbers and switching games off. So the graphics should feel like a dark control room: screens, maps and numbers glowing in the dark, then switching off.

It fits what you've already locked:
- the warm-white serif captions with a soft glow
- the halftone texture
- slow push-ins

**The rules that keep it consistent and not "AI-looking":**
- **Palette:** black background, warm white (#FFF9F0), plus one accent: soft amber, used only for things that are *alive* (the 5 hits, Canada/Australia, the one city). Everything dead goes grey.
- **Type:** Instrument Serif for words and big numbers (same as the captions). Use a small mono (IBM Plex Mono) only for labels and data such as "SOFT LAUNCH", "DEC 2014" or "DAU 12,408".
- **Texture:** halftone and a slight CRT bloom on every graphic, so it sits with the footage rather than looking like a flat template.
- **Motion:**
  - Things light up and switch off. They don't fly in.
  - Slow camera push on every graphic, the same as your talking-head zooms.
  - Hard cuts between beats; the zoom-through transitions stay on the talking head.
  - No bounce, no spin, no 3D globe whoosh.

## If you only build three
1. **01 + 06: one continuous map.** World, then Canada/Australia, later one country, then one city. One After Effects comp reused three times.
2. **02: game graveyard.** It's the hook graphic in the first 4 seconds.
3. **03: CRT shut-off** on "By February, it was gone."

## How to build them (see YouTube #09–#14)
- **Map:** the Netflix-doc map tutorial (#09); GeoLayers works too. Dot-matrix look: fill land with a dot grid and mask it with country shapes.
- **Graveyard grid:** the dynamic grids tutorial (#13). Drive each tile's opacity with a staggered delay.
- **Numbers:** rolling numbers (#11).
- **CRT off:** the reusable TV on/off effect (#10).
- **VHS date stamp:** the camcorder effect (#14).
