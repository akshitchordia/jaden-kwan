# Style lock: Supercell world (B-rolls for the Supercell reel)

From now on, every new Supercell-world B-roll should look like it came from the same studio as these 24 references (`00 Supercell world MOODBOARD.jpg`; files and Pinterest links in `references/`).

## The look in one line
Chunky, hand-sculpted 3D cartoon characters with big expressive faces, rendered like a Pixar short: soft global illumination, warm rim light, saturated but never neon, and a slightly painterly, cinematic finish.

## Characters
- **Proportions:** big heads and hands, small legs, thick and chunky silhouettes. Readable at thumbnail size.
- **Faces:** huge eyebrows, exaggerated expressions (grit, rage, smug grin, wide-eyed worry). See 04, 07, 17.
- **Materials:** matte clay-like skin with subsurface warmth, chunky brushed metal (PEKKA, cannon), soft fabric and fur, wood with rounded edges. No photoreal pores.
- **Turntable look (10–14):** the character stands on a small square chunk of grass and soil, on a soft grey studio backdrop, with a soft top key light and a contact shadow. Use this for any "hero intro" or "game card" shot.

## World and colour
- **Clash (CoC, Royale):** warm golds, grass green, stone grey, royal purple and blue accents. Gold glow on loot, orange fire, purple elixir.
- **Boom Beach:** turquoise sea, bright tropical greens, military blue and khaki.
- **Hay Day:** sunny pastels, barn red, wheat yellow, soft blue sky.
- **Brawl Stars:** punchier, higher-contrast colour on clean white or flat backgrounds.
- **Skies:** big volumetric clouds, golden-hour or dramatic stormy light (02, 05, 06).

## Lighting and camera
- **Cinematic shots (02–07):** low hero angles, shallow depth of field, rim light from behind, atmospheric haze, dust and embers in the air.
- **Lens:** about 35–50 mm for characters, wide for villages and battlefields seen from above (05, 09).
- **Motion (for Kling and Seedance):** slow push-ins and orbits, characters with snappy, weighty, cartoon timing (anticipation, then a hit). No floaty AI drift.

## How it fits the reel's "control room at night" direction
The talking head and the graphics stay dark and moody, so the Supercell world should enter *through screens and light*:
- characters glowing on CRTs or phones in the dark
- a turntable character lit only by a screen
- a bright Clash village revealed as the camera pushes into a monitor

Keep the Supercell colours inside the frame-within-a-frame, and keep everything around it dark.

## Copy-paste style anchor (add to every image or video prompt)
```
Supercell game art style: chunky stylised 3D cartoon characters with big heads, oversized hands and huge expressive eyebrows, hand-sculpted clay-like materials, Pixar-quality render, soft global illumination with warm rim light, saturated warm colour palette (gold, grass green, royal purple, sky blue), volumetric clouds and atmospheric haze, cinematic low hero angle, shallow depth of field.
```
**Negative:** photoreal skin, anime, thin realistic proportions, flat vector, neon cyberpunk colours, extra text or logos, misspelled game titles.

## Note on the references
Most images are official Supercell key art or renders. A few (09, 15, 20, 21) are high-quality fan or AI pieces kept for mood and composition. Don't copy their logos or text.
